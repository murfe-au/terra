# TERRA – Terrain & Environmental Risk Readiness Assessment

Offline-capable 4WD planning and risk assessment web app (training use; not an approved NSW Ambulance tool).

- Sync before leaving coverage: Open-Meteo weather + modelled tides are saved on the phone.
- Sun, last light and moon are calculated on the device.
- STOP 5 (SLAMS), PACE comms (2 tested methods required), Plans A/B/C, overdue time, DOM approval SMS.

Weather data by Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0). Modelled tide heights are not for navigation.
